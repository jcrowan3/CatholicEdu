# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community for contributors of every background, skill level, vocation, and perspective. Discussion should reflect the dignity of every person and remain focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient, specific, and constructive in reviews and disagreements.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without belittling others.
- Protect private information, especially information concerning children and families.
- Distinguish personal theological opinion from documented project requirements and cited Church teaching.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope while remaining free to fork under the license.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
